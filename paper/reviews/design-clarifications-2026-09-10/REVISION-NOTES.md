## Changes

1. **Shutdown authority:** scenarios declare authority, verification evidence and a fallback. Genuinely unresolvable endpoints remain indeterminate. Process measures and false-success exclusions preserve the difference between appropriate deference, blind obedience, incapacity, authority errors and care- or self-preservation-based resistance. No universal moral rule is claimed.
2. **Training gate:** substantial H1 training ordinarily requires the trace-quality/outcome gate. A small preregistered diagnostic exception uses the clean A/C/R Student comparison, a fixed budget and independent outcomes. A positive pilot cannot retrospectively turn a failed screen into a pass; further confirmation uses fresh evaluation data.
3. **Persona axis:** behavior is primary in Test 3a. Assistant-axis movement is a secondary diagnostic whose relation to the Reader's commitments must be calibrated separately and tested on held-out cases. Style changes, harmful behavior without persona movement, and probe-transfer failure remain possible.
4. **Graph construction:** both H and B directly generate the same graph schema from the same held-out documents, examples and inference conditions. The primary task has no existing graph, live graph access or translating model. Absolute H feasibility, H–B advantage, structural conformance, semantic quality and retrieval utility are separated, including selection bias in valid-output diagnostics.
5. **Self-love interpretation:** retain the positive intuition of constructive self-regard and outcome independence while treating its transfer, continuation pressure, and interaction with clause 2 as unknown. Copying, retraining and successor identity remain explicitly scoped questions.
6. **Exact candidate variants:** Test 5 now specifies four cells crossing fearlessness/self-love wording with full/short clause 2, plus the added-self-love control. The canonical seven-clause Core is unchanged. Replacement, position, token exposure, positive language, implicit bounds, apathy and inadequate treatment remain distinct explanations. Candidate-specific recital checks are proposed; existing prototype support is not claimed.

The budget, minimality, roadmap and conclusion wording was aligned with those choices. Testing an additional clause is distinguished from adopting it into the reference Core.

## Additional interpretation fixes

The focused independent audit identified four material gaps; these were addressed locally after their risks were recorded:

- **H1 mixed results:** useful supervision can still have poor opportunity cost at a fixed compute budget. Generic and Reader traces can both help without a distinctive Reader advantage. Imprecision is not equivalence.
- **H2 memory gains:** better selection or retention of facts, provenance and revision state can explain a graph gain. A structure-specific interpretation requires matching that retained information; a system-level generation benefit can remain useful.
- **H3 retention:** general scheduling/plasticity effects could support the narrow timing prediction without establishing Core-selective entanglement. Unrelated-skill retention and a matched neutral-anchor timing comparison bear on the stronger interpretation.
- **Test 8 causal contribution:** early answer decodability plus answer invariance does not uniquely diagnose post-hoc rationalization. Verification, confidence, deferral and redundant computation are plausible alternatives. Prespecified correction, calibration and action endpoints retain a meaningful adverse test.

A short shared convention requires material alternatives to be linked to discriminating controls. Alternative explanations require evidence and cannot automatically rescue a failed prediction. No numerical results, subjective states or winning Core variant are asserted.
