Citation keys, headings, labels, original URLs, and figures were checked against the originals. No bibliography entries or observed pipeline artifacts were edited. This pass leaves philosophical and historical passages in the paper. It does not compress authentic care into behavioral scores, settle unresolved moral questions, remove named tests, or substitute a narrow timing study for the full Reader-Core vision. The additional precision modestly increases length; a shorter edition would require a separate, cumulative preservation review.
