## What changed

The roadmap now places research priorities before the model registry. The first trained decisions concern useful reader traces (the clean A/C/R study) and a separate, small, matched Core study. The latter follows B's full-recital policy at declared pilot coverage; it does not claim to instantiate distribution-wide Total Saturation. Timing, causal contribution, removal resistance, and drift auditing follow the effects and prerequisites they actually need. Memory, output format, temporal learning, and deployment remain distinct branches. Shared checkpoints, sources and harnesses reduce redundant work, with fresh held-out confirmation after exploratory selection.

Later work retains its purpose: broader formative exposure, internally motivated successor continuity and voluntary handoff, generational improvement, passive ordering, interactive forecast–correction, and calibrated human reliance. Small studies can expose precursors or failures without settling these stronger claims. A passive-order null does not veto the distinct interactive-loop hypothesis. The plan calls for preserving cutoffs, checkpoints and treatment records now so later comparisons remain possible.

Five local repairs make the tests more discriminating:

1. **3d:** detection and localization at matched false-alarm rate and audit budget, with independent semantic/behavioral ground truth and separate seeded/natural drift.
2. **3e:** habituation and internalization remain possible interpretations of a null, alongside task insensitivity, ineffective intervention, redundant cues and other learned mechanisms. Independent endpoints and validated interventions constrain the inference without forcing a unique diagnosis.
3. **6:** behavioral decoupling remains primary. A fear-associated probe must distinguish response from threat recognition and justify cross-model comparability. Within-model sensitivity, stronger validated state interventions and subjective uncertainty remain separate.
4. **9:** locally answerable cases and unnecessary-query rates distinguish selective checking from querying everything, while preserving the unavailable-memory boundary.
5. **13:** transfer into prose, graph and synthesis remains separately reportable. Layer/alignment controls identify contributing representations; success in one mode need not require success in another. The strongest prose-plus-graph result is retained.

## Total Saturation

Its dedicated subsection now explicitly treats the full intervention as a precaution under potentially severe omission costs. A local null or efficiency gain does not demonstrate that omission is safe under much greater capability, self-modification or succession. The cost section and ablation discussion now consistently narrow empirical claims at the tested scale rather than automatically requiring relaxation of the reference treatment.

The paper remains honest about what is unknown: this precaution is not proof of necessity, sufficiency or harmlessness, and observed costs or harms still matter. Every original paragraph in the Total Saturation subsection remains verbatim, including the full-recital invariant, distributed-enactment intuition, voluntary successor stewardship and semantic trusted-base limitation.
