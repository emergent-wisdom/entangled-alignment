# Source and rights

Franz Kafka, *The Metamorphosis*, translated by Ian Johnston. The Kafka readings in this folder used the exact text in `metamorphosis-johnston.txt`. It has 122,332 UTF-16 code units and SHA-256 `e06af7cc1d2647ccc5bffd8d4edfd491198b2b7a2422f85f1a142b02c5493950`.

The source was extracted from the Vancouver Island University repository PDF of *The Metamorphosis and Other Stories*: https://hdl.handle.net/10613/28253 (DOI https://doi.org/10.25316/IR-19858). The PDF's SHA-256 is `ff61ba371e8e70738cfcd99b9c3732219a34382bfd93b3767d373ea7cfd277e6`. Page 1 states: “This document is in the public domain.” This identifies that exact edition; it does not assert the same rights for every Johnston edition or the older HTML translation.

The story occupies PDF pages 2–50. Front matter and other stories were omitted; the II and III headings, spelling and punctuation were retained. The source contains no editorial corrections. Extraction records preserve the page lines, paragraph joins and the uncertainty at page boundaries. `rights-and-source.json` and `provenance.json` identify the source and preparation.
