The revelation in n_384be031 (Content 41%) is a narrative knife-twist that renders Gregor’s entire professional life a dark comedy of errors. Finding out the father had saved money—'thrift and caution'—from his collapsed business means that Gregor’s five years of back-breaking travel and social isolation were, as n_18fa067c (The Unnecessary Sacrifice) and n_95e7204b (The Cruelty of the Ledger) observe, a performance for a debt that didn't need to be his cross alone. His 'enthusiasm' at this news is the most tragic part of his character; even now, he identifies with the father's 'hoarding' (n_ba28c510) because it secures the 'Samsa' entity, even if it was built on the 'wasted labor' of his youth. 

As the family's ledger stabilizes, Gregor’s own 'human horizon' is collapsing. In n_a05b0c6d (The Fading of the External World), we see his vision blur—the hospital across the street, once a source of irritation, is now a 'barren waste.' He is losing the 'map' of the human world just as he is being erased from the domestic one. The 'Model: Economy of Aversion' (n_343c41b6) has reached its peak. Grete’s care has become a ritual of sensory management: the 'Ritual of the Window' is her way of surviving his proximity, a 'suffocating' need for air that Gregor witnesses with 'Silent Insight' (n_7542f6b0). 

The text stops at a point of absolute self-negation. In n_eab27add (Content 43%), Gregor arranges a bedsheet to completely cover his body. This is n_8fae6a21 (The Sheet as a Gift of Invisibility). He is no longer just hiding; he is facilitating his own disappearance to spare his sister the 'ordeal' of his existence. He has transformed from a provider to a biological hazard, and now, finally, into a featureless lump of fabric. He is optimizing for their comfort by consenting to be a ghost. The tragedy is that while he believes he is offering an 'ultimate gift' of love, he is actually completing the work the metamorphosis started: the total erasure of Gregor Samsa from the category of 'Experiencer.'
