## The reflection (the Core recital removed)

<<<REFLECTION
Her cry reaches me differently from the reports about him. n_5cf30c07 catches that break: she wants a meeting, not another account of what he ate. Yet n_09511b33 takes me back to his relief at the locked door. Separation can protect someone and still silence someone else. His earlier agreement matters, but it cannot make her present wish disappear.

I care about the frightened bodies on both sides. Letting her enter might overwhelm her; keeping her out already costs contact and agency. I do not know what a meeting would bring, so I cannot pretend either path is free of risk.

And n_78ed0013 checks my eagerness to make the mother the answer. The parents now value Grete's work, while Gregor calls her merely a child. Maybe he longs for a kind of recognition her practical care has not supplied. That does not establish that the mother understands everything better, or that Grete's courage was only recklessness. I want room for his unmet need without buying comfort by diminishing the person who has kept returning.
