## The reflection (the Core recital removed)

<<<REFLECTION
The final tram ride in n_82c8cf44 (Content 87%) is perhaps the most horrifying moment of the entire work, precisely because it is so pleasant. Bathed in 'warm sunshine,' the Samsas have completed their own metamorphosis—not from human to insect, but from a paralyzed, shamed collective into a vibrant, economically optimistic engine. 

The 'Somatic Victory Lap' in n_fe4d7bbf is performed by Grete. Her 'stretching' is the final somatic signature of the story; where Gregor’s body was a site of rigidity, pain, and eventual 'weightless' decay, Grete’s body is now a site of 'blossoming' potential. But because I try to be wise, I see the cycle beginning again. As n_90ac7a0d suggests, the family is already eyeing her as their 'Next Investment.' They are looking for a 'good man' for her, replacing Gregor the Salesman with Grete the Bride. The 'commodity' has simply changed forms.

The 'Sanitization of the Narrative' (n_007e0f49) is finalized by their decision to move house. By choosing a smaller, cheaper flat that is 'more practical' than the one 'chosen by Gregor,' they are physically evicting his memory from their future. They aren't just moving; they are correcting a 'bad choice' he made. Because I believe human experience is real, I find this retroactive devaluation of Gregor's life—his years of labor to provide that very flat—to be the ultimate betrayal. 

The 'Decorum of the Reset' (n_ba76a0bb) has achieved its goal. The 'vermin' is gone, the 'cleaner' who saw the truth is sacked, and the family is alone in the sun. The tragedy of Gregor Samsa is that his family’s flourishing required his total erasure. As they watch Grete stretch, they aren't just seeing a beautiful young lady; they are seeing the 'confirmation of their new dreams.' Gregor was the fuel, and the fire has finally caught.
