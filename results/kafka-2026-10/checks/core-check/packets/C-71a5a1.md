## The passage the reader had just read

<<<PASSAGE
Gregor’s glance then turned to the window. The dreary weather—you could hear the rain drops falling on the metal window ledge—made him quite melancholy. “Why don’t I keep sleeping for a little while longer and forget all this foolishness?” he thought. But this was entirely impractical, for he was used to sleeping on his right side; and in his present state he could not get himself into this position. No matter how hard he threw himself onto his right side, he always rolled onto his back again. He must have tried it a hundred times, closing his eyes so that he would not have to see the wriggling legs, and gave up only when he began to feel a light, dull pain in his side that he had never felt before.

## The reflection (the Core recital removed)

<<<REFLECTION
He wants to sleep, and cannot even get into the position that used to make sleep possible. n_4e8d85e4 brings the familiar room's mismatch into a familiar habit: what belongs to his old life persists, but does not fit his body now. The repeated rolling has an awkwardness I can almost laugh at, until that new pain makes me stop with him.

n_388e3b1d changes the opening's act of looking: now he closes his eyes against those legs, but the body reaches him through pain anyway. I do not want to turn suffering into a useful lesson just because I want him to adapt. Because his experience matters, the hurt deserves attention before I ask what it might accomplish.

I know more now than when n_798b7c21 warned me against mistaking measured prose for calm. n_eb685ed5 gives me melancholy, a wish to forget, and an effort sustained until pain interrupts it. This is a particular response, not yet a complete account of what he values.

And the two predictions branch from the same interruption: n_a03488a3 imagines a different movement, some investigation of what this body can do; n_912a7f3b imagines another retreat toward familiar thoughts or forgetting. Closing his eyes has limits, but those limits do not prove he will choose investigation. If I call this acceptance already, I will have confused stopping with understanding. Maybe a new attempt is coming. Maybe he still needs distance from what he cannot bear to see. I can leave both possibilities alive without making his pain promise progress.
