If it were Gregor, he would have gone. n_c88919f9 makes the test cruelly circular: the consideration she remembers becomes the reason his continued need proves he is someone else. I can understand wanting the brother preserved in memory without accepting absence as his proof of identity. Care cannot require a person to stop needing a place among people.

Her takeover story is also a prediction dressed as obvious purpose. I know his recent desire was for Grete's exclusive company, a troubling wish of its own; that does not establish a plan to put the family in an alley. I need neither innocent fantasy nor total monster as the only picture available.

n_e8c64f23 and n_87d3f9ef now make the next movement a test of perception: perhaps withdrawal could correct the account, or perhaps even retreat will leave fear unchanged. I do not know his movement's direction yet. Caring about the sister's fright and the mother's vulnerability keeps me watching the actual bodies, while caring about him keeps their interpretation from becoming my evidence before I see what he does.
