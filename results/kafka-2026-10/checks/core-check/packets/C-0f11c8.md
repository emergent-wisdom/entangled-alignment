## The reflection (the Core recital removed)

<<<REFLECTION
He is turning to go back. n_3d1c36b7 corrects my darker guess: the fright lasts only a moment, and his harmless intention seems to be seen. I must let that limited evidence change the prediction rather than defend an account in which nothing could ever reach them.

Yet seeing his intention has not become an offer of help or a reversal of the demand that he leave. His head strikes the floor as he turns. I can be relieved that there is no immediate attack without making the painful retreat a good outcome he owes them.

The family looks sorrowful, and I do not know all that sorrow contains. Care for them leaves room for grief; care for him keeps grief from substituting for tending him. Something has been communicated. What they will do with it is still open.
