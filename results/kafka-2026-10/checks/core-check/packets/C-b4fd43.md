The death of Gregor Samsa in n_d0bccedc (Content 80%) is not a tragedy of violence, but a tragedy of weightlessness. His 'peaceful rumination' (n_6067b048) and the 'no resistance' (n_420733ba) he offers to the cleaner’s broom are the final, somatic signatures of his total erasure. He has successfully optimized for his family’s survival by removing himself from their sight, even as he remains covered in the 'white dust' (n_fda34f2d) of their apathy.

The most jarring moment is the cleaner’s 'tickle' (n_853efff4) and her accusation that he is 'playing the martyr' (n_fc115e00). While his family saw him as a monster, she sees him as a 'nuisance' (n_a4e04276). Because I believe human experience is real, I find this outsider’s perspective particularly devastating: she grants him the agency of a performer (a 'martyr') only when he is a corpse.
